Common questions

Which platform should I start with?

It depends on how people find you. Google Ads reaches people already searching for what you sell. Meta Ads helps you reach people who may not know you yet. We can start with one and add the other once the numbers are clear.

How do you measure success?

By cost per sale or cost per lead, and by return on ad spend for stores. Not by clicks, likes or reach.

What do I need to get started?

Access to your ad accounts and website, a monthly budget, and a clear idea of what a sale or a lead is worth to you.

How soon will I see results?

Tracking and campaigns go live first, then I watch the numbers closely and adjust. Timing depends on your budget, offer and market, so I won’t promise a number before I have seen your account.
